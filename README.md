## About

Founder of **[ajtak.it](https://ajtak.it)** — freelance software development & IT services based in Czech Republic. I design, build, deploy, and maintain full-stack applications from scratch on my own infrastructure.

- 7+ years in IT, building since age 14
- 20+ projects delivered — from SaaS platforms to enterprise IT for 120+ branch offices
- Self-hosted everything on 2x Proxmox nodes with 60+ containers
- Currently focused on **Next.js 16**, **Flutter**, **Supabase**, and **AI automation**
